Hangul has **19 consonants**: 14 basic ones and 5 tense ones. The basic set is **ㄱ, ㄴ, ㄷ, ㄹ, ㅁ, ㅂ, ㅅ, ㅇ, ㅈ, ㅊ, ㅋ, ㅌ, ㅍ, and ㅎ**. The tense versions are **ㄲ, ㄸ, ㅃ, ㅆ, and ㅉ**. Many shapes mimic mouth position or tongue placement.

Skip trying to learn all 19 together.

Focus on the three-way split: **plain**, **aspirated**, and **tense**. Plain consonants (ㄱ, ㄷ, ㅂ, ㅈ) sound softer than their romanization. Aspirated ones (ㅋ, ㅌ, ㅍ, ㅊ) release air clearly. Tense ones (ㄲ, ㄸ, ㅃ, ㅆ, ㅉ) tighten the throat. The cards map each shape to sound, romanization, IPA, and an example.

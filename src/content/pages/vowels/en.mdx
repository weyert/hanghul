Hangul has **21 vowels**: 10 basic and 11 compound. The basic ones are **ㅏ, ㅑ, ㅓ, ㅕ, ㅗ, ㅛ, ㅜ, ㅠ, ㅡ, and ㅣ**. Compounds stack or combine these into **ㅐ, ㅔ, ㅘ, ㅝ, ㅢ, ㅚ** and more. The vowel also determines the block shape—whether it sits side by side or stacked.

Two—ㅡ and ㅓ—have no English equivalent. The rest land somewhere familiar.

Start with the core four: open **ㅏ /a/**, open unrounded **ㅓ /eo/**, rounded **ㅗ /o/**, and **ㅜ /u/**. Add the y-vowels (ㅑ, ㅕ, ㅛ, ㅠ) next. Save compounds for later.
